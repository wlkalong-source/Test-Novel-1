Chapter 16

  


  


Something itched. I reached up to scratch behind my ear, but couldn’t move my arm and when I pried my eyes open, I saw a clear tube filled with yellow bubbles leading from my head to a bag attached to the side of the bed. The nurse noticed I was awake, warned me “don’t scratch” and rubbed gel around the area, which stopped the itching, then she untied my wrists.

I was in the infirmary and it felt like I’d been strapped to a mattress filled with rocks. A number of marines were in the beds next to me, most in worse shape than I. Many were missing limbs and the women next to me had lost an arm and both of her legs below the knees. I recognized her as one of the heavy pilots who’d had to leave her powered armor behind and make it through the battle wearing only her flight suit. I tried to lift my head to look around, but the medication made me woozy and soon feel back asleep.

The sound of voices woke me up again and I saw Captain Parcu making the rounds, spending a little time with each of his soldiers. He patted the unconscious women next to me gently on the shoulder, then stopped at my bed.

“I’m glad to see you Michael. Everyone thought you were dead.”

“I don’t remember anything.”

“You can thank the Spree for your rescue. After you fell, the Takers dragged you way and the Spree were able to locate you because of your button. We found you in narrow ravine, a kilometer away from the landing site with a couple of Takers.”

“How long have I been here?”

“You’ve been here two days. Coverella’s been keeping a close eye on your progress when she’s not on duty. The captain had to assign her extra work to keep her away. She really seems to have it in for her.”

“What exactly did she do?”

“I can’t talk about it. Lets just say one of the injured parties was close to the captain. I don’t know if she will ever get over it.”

“What about Hanes?”

“He didn’t make it. They jumped him and one of the marines hit him in the head trying to kill the Takers on top of him. It wasn’t his fault. They’d already pulled off one of Hanes’ arms and would have killed him anyway.”

“Could you tell me what happened?”

“The ammo you brought back saved us. We were able to hold the Takers off long enough for the fleet to break through and once we had air cover the Takers stood no chance. The few that survived escaped with the remains of their fleet. We’re pursuing, but won’t catch them before they reach the jump point. They have too much of a head start. We couldn’t leave orbit until we cleared the base and rescued the marines at the bottom of the shaft. I’m still not sure how any of them survived.”

“I’m glad to hear that. I know you lost a lot of men.”

Parcu nodded and briefly closed his eyes, then looked back up at me.

“It’s part of the job. Part of the job I hate. I need to check on the rest of my troops. I just wanted to thank you personally for what you did. You acquitted yourself quite well.”

“I was terrified the whole time.”

“I know. I could hear it in your voice. You kept going in spite of the fear. That’s courage.”

“Thanks. I don’t feel courageous.”

Parcu shook my hand, then moved to the next bunk. Seeing the soldier with no legs reminded me of Sarah, and I was glad for the sedatives. It had been more than two years since I’d found her body in the tunnels on Miras, but the image never left me.

The lights in the infirmary hurt my eyes, so I squeezed them shut and tried to fall back asleep, and could feel my mind start to wander, when I felt somebody lean over and kiss me on the forehead. and I knew by the tropical smell it was Coverella. , and I knew them and tried to relax so I could fall back asleep when I felt somebody kiss me on the forehead, and I knew by smell of sandalwood it was Coverella. I don’t think she realized I was conscious because she looked startled when I opened my eyes and pulled away to wipe the tears from her eyes.

“I’m sorry. I thought you were still asleep.”

“I heard you’d been around.”

“I thought you were dead. I didn’t know what to do.” She wiped a few more tears from her eyes and bit her lip. “I haven’t slept in three days and the captain put me on double duty when you went missing. I don’t know if she did it to keep my mind off you or just to be cruel. You weren’t supposed to see me cry.”

“It’s OK. I was a little worried about you as well. I heard you had quite a battle (how would I know this unless Parku says something above??.”

“Yes. We lost a number of Corvettes and one of the destroyers took a lot of damage but we didn’t lose any of the capital ships. I spent the whole battle in Ops as a relay officer. The captain refused to reassign me to another ship. She still doesn’t trust me.”

“I don’t trust you either. Stealing kisses from people you think are unconscious isn’t exactly honorable behavior.”

“How did you know?”

“I didn’t. I was just teasing you.”

“The doc said you’ll be released tomorrow. I’ll be glad to have you back.”

“Cov. did you hear if they found any of the crew from the survey ship. I forgot to ask Parcu when he was here. I’m having a hard time focusing. It was the first thing I wanted to ask him but I forgot.”

Coverella stepped a little way back and removed her hand from my arm.

“No. They didn’t find any sign of where they took them. They did find the remains of two of the crew. They’d been beaten to death.”

I reached out and grabbed her hand.

“Thanks for taking care of me. I’m sorry this is so hard on you. I wish things were different, but they aren’t. You mean a lot to me.”

I could see tears well up in her eyes.

“I have to go. My second shift starts in a few minutes.”

She took a few steps towards the door, then ran back and hugged me.

“I’ll see you tomorrow. I’m so glad your going to be OK.”
